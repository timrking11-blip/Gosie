---
name: Reviewed lead approval boundary
description: Why manual source-lead edits require renewed independent review rather than updating a public listing directly.
---

Manual public-source leads use independent review: editing an approved entry hides it until a different moderator approves the changed facts. Keep them separate from fictional ranked samples and transient provider search results.

**Why:** Approval of old evidence must not silently authorize new source details, costs, travel estimates, or availability claims. Manual curation is not provider verification or a ticket-availability guarantee.

**How to apply:** Preserve this boundary when adding import tools, bulk edits, alternate clients, or richer discovery workflows. Unknown facts must remain unknown rather than receiving invented values.
