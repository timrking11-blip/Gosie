description: People-first purpose and organization participation boundary.
---

Gosie Communities is for people. Venues, municipalities and nonprofits may contribute useful local knowledge alongside individual contributors and restaurants, but the product must not become an advertising directory.

**Why:** The user requested expanding participation to these organizations and explicitly stressed that it is “for the people.”

**How to apply:** Keep people-first language in public copy and submission guidance. Organization participation does not imply self-publication, verified identity or preferential ranking; retain moderator review and practical community benefit as the standard.

Organization access and historical contribution quality are separate judgments.
Revocation alone must not be treated as evidence that earlier information is wrong.

**Why:** Useful local knowledge can remain useful after representation changes.
The re-review scope explicitly preserves historical attribution and visibility
until a separate moderator acts; it does not authorize blanket punishment.

**How to apply:** Keep future access-control changes separate from explicit,
people-first content review. Do not silently turn revocation into bulk removal,
publication, or a public accusation against an organization.
