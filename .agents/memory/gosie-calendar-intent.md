---
name: Calendar-aware launch intent
description: Product requirement for calendar-aware pilot specifications; not authorization to connect accounts.
---

Gosie's launch specification should account for existing leisure, sports and book-club calendar events. Calendar-file export alone does not meet the requested experience.

**Why:** The user clarified that calendars track activities already planned and rejected a launch scope that treated calendar support only as an export button.

**How to apply:** Include calendar-aware commitments and availability in specification discussions. Do not treat this product direction as permission to connect accounts, read calendar data, activate providers, write events, invite participants or publish. Provider choice and per-user consent remain explicit decisions.
