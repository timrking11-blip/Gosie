The pilot cohort is five invited participants plus one additional account for
the product owner, six accounts total.

**Why:** The owner explicitly requested an extra pilot place to use the product
alongside the testers rather than taking one of their five places.

**How to apply:** Include the owner in the invitation and consent process, without
granting administrator, operator or moderator powers. The extra place does not
authorize publishing, public registration or production account creation.
